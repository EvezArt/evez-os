# Arsenal Ledger

The Arsenal Ledger is a public-interest accountability layer for EVEZ-OS.

Its objective is not to "destroy" an institution by sabotage. It is to make opaque flows of money and influence easier to inspect, reproduce, challenge, and explain.

## Public-data spine

The initial source registry is built around:
- USAspending federal award/spending records
- SAM.gov contracting and opportunity records
- DOJ FARA public registrations
- FEC campaign-finance disclosures

The system preserves the source name, source URI, input digest, row digest, normalized record digest, and generated result digest.

## Signals

Examples:
- recipient concentration
- repeated counterparties
- sole-source conditions
- entity-agency relationships

A signal is not an allegation.

The engine explicitly emits:

SIGNAL != ALLEGATION != PROOF

An analyst still has to inspect the underlying documents, procurement rules, dates, amendments, competition, exemptions, and surrounding evidence.

## Why this can bite

A conventional dashboard says:
"Company X received $Y."

A graph-and-evidence system can ask:
"How concentrated is the recipient relationship?"
"How persistent is it?"
"Which agencies are involved?"
"Which awards and transactions produce the number?"
"Can the source record be independently reproduced?"
"Which public filings sit in the same time window?"
"What remains unknown?"

The objective is to make claims expensive to fake and cheap to audit.

## Nonviolent boundary

This system is designed for:
- public-record research
- investigative journalism
- procurement oversight
- anti-corruption research
- policy analysis
- civic accountability

It does not provide sabotage, intrusion, targeting, weapons guidance, or methods for disrupting physical infrastructure.

The lever is evidence.
